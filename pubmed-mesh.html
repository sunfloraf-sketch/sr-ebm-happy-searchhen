使用提示Tips

  • 主題詞（含下位詞）[mh] 會自動包含樹狀結構中所有下位（狹義）標目；僅此標目 [mh:noexp] 則不含。主要主題 [majr] 只找以此為文章重點的文獻。
  • Entry Terms 常是倒裝形式（例：Attack, Heart），勾選「倒裝詞轉為自然語序」後會轉成 Heart Attack，才能在題名／摘要中比對到。
  • 鄰近字：每列可設定一次，套用到該列所有兩個字以上的自由詞，例："hip fracture"[tiab:~3] 會找到 fracture of the hip。PubMed 鄰近字只支援題名［ti］與題名／摘要［tiab］，且不能與截字 * 併用。
  • 出版類型 [pt]：研究設計欄位加入 Randomized Controlled Trial、Meta-Analysis 等標目時預設為 [pt]（例："Randomized Controlled Trial"[pt]）。請同時放自由詞（randomized、placebo），以免漏掉尚未標引的新文獻。
  • 比較（C）若與介入相同概念，通常不必納入 AND 組合，可取消勾選「納入」。
  • MeSH, incl. narrower [mh] explodes to every narrower heading in the tree; this heading only [mh:noexp] does not. Major topic [majr] finds articles where it is a main focus.
  • Entry Terms are often inverted (e.g. Attack, Heart). With "Un-invert entry terms" ticked they become Heart Attack, so they match titles/abstracts.
  • Proximity: set once per row; it applies to every multi-word free-text term in that row, e.g. "hip fracture"[tiab:~3] also finds fracture of the hip. PubMed proximity works only in [ti] and [tiab], and not with truncation *.
  • Publication type [pt]: in Study design, headings such as Randomized Controlled Trial or Meta-Analysis default to [pt] (e.g. "Randomized Controlled Trial"[pt]). Add free-text too (randomized, placebo) to catch not-yet-indexed records.
  • If the Comparison duplicates the Intervention concept, untick "Include" so it is not ANDed.
