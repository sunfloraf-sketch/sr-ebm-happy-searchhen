① PICO ＋ 研究設計① PICO + Study design

在任一列輸入英文檢索詞並從候選清單選擇 MeSH 標目，會同時加入控制辭彙與其 Entry Terms；點標籤可調整欄位或增減詞彙。同一列內以 OR 連結，右上角有勾選的列（加入檢索策略）之間以 AND 組合。PICO 只是引導框架：列的代號（如 P1）與名稱可直接點選修改，也可新增或刪除概念列。Type an English term in any row and pick a MeSH heading — the heading and its Entry Terms are added together; click a tag to change its field or terms. Terms within a row are ORed; rows ticked at the top right (added to the strategy) are ANDed. PICO is only a guide: click a row's code (e.g. P1) or name to rename it, and add or delete rows as needed.

使用提示Tips

  • 控制辭彙（含狹義詞）[mh] 會自動包含樹狀結構中所有下位（狹義）標目；不含狹義詞 [mh:noexp] 則只找此標目。重點控制辭彙 [majr] 只找以此為文章重點的文獻。
  • Entry Terms 常是倒裝形式（例：Attack, Heart），加入時會自動轉成自然語序 Heart Attack，才能在題名／摘要中比對到。
  • 鄰近字：每列可設定一次，套用到該列所有兩個字以上的自由詞彙，例："hip fracture"[tiab:~3] 會找到 fracture of the hip。PubMed 鄰近字只支援篇名/摘要 [tiab] 與篇名 [ti]；選所有欄位 [all] 或文字欄位 [tw] 時不套用，且不能與截字 * 併用。
  • 出版類型 [pt]：研究設計欄位加入 Randomized Controlled Trial、Meta-Analysis 等標目時預設為 [pt]（例："Randomized Controlled Trial"[pt]）。請同時放自由詞彙（randomized、placebo），以免漏掉尚未標引的新文獻。
  • 比較（C）若與介入相同概念，通常不必納入 AND 組合，可取消該列右上角的勾選。
  • MeSH (incl. narrower) [mh] explodes to every narrower heading in the tree; no narrower [mh:noexp] searches this heading only. Major MeSH [majr] finds articles where it is a main focus.
  • Entry Terms are often inverted (e.g. Attack, Heart); they are automatically converted to natural order (Heart Attack) so they match titles/abstracts.
  • Proximity: set once per row; it applies to every multi-word free-text term in that row, e.g. "hip fracture"[tiab:~3] also finds fracture of the hip. PubMed proximity works only in [tiab] and [ti] (not applied with [all] / [tw]), and not with truncation *.
  • Publication type [pt]: in Study design, headings such as Randomized Controlled Trial or Meta-Analysis default to [pt] (e.g. "Randomized Controlled Trial"[pt]). Add free-text too (randomized, placebo) to catch not-yet-indexed records.
  • If the Comparison duplicates the Intervention concept, untick the box at the top right of that row so it is not ANDed.
