系統分堆規則How the auto-split works

  • 重複出現才算核心字：在 2 行以上出現的字才進詞堆。
  • 括號註記忽略：Careflow (central venous catheter) 只取 Careflow。
  • 逗號不拆詞：catheter, central venous 自動轉正。
  • 頭尾修飾字略過：kit、short-term 只出現一次又在頭尾，自動拿掉。
  • 較短片語不分堆：少於堆數的詞（central line）以片語併入。
  • 泛用詞那堆可拿掉：整堆只有 disease、disorder、dysfunction、patient 這類泛用詞，或夾在中間的治療／情境用語（therapy、treatment、related），詞堆上方會建議「移除」或「合併」。
  • 可直接貼檢索式：(GDM OR "gestational diabetes mellitus" OR "gestational diabetes"…)，將依大寫 OR 拆成一行一詞。
  • 結構混合時分組：同時有兩詞（critical care）與三詞（respiratory care unit）的情況時，會多一張「二堆＋三堆（分組）」：兩組各自分堆再 OR 合併。
  • 短字不切截、自動補複數：切截後不足 5 個字母（vein、line、cath）就不切，避免 cath* 這類切太前面；4 個字母以內的字自動補複數。
  • Core words must recur: only words found in 2+ lines go into the stacks.
  • Bracketed notes ignored: Careflow (central venous catheter) → Careflow.
  • Commas don’t split terms: catheter, central venous is put back in order.
  • Edge qualifiers skipped: one-off words at the start or end (kit, short-term) are dropped.
  • Shorter phrases aren’t stacked: terms with fewer words than stacks (central line) are OR-ed in as phrases.
  • A generic stack can go: if a whole stack is just generic words (disease, disorder, dysfunction, patient) or treatment/context words in the middle (therapy, treatment, related), a suggestion above the stacks offers “Remove” or “Merge”.
  • Paste a search string: (GDM OR "gestational diabetes mellitus" OR "gestational diabetes"…) is split by uppercase OR into one term per line.
  • Mixed structures → groups: with both 2-word (critical care) and 3-word (respiratory care unit) terms, an extra “2 + 3 stacks (grouped)” option appears: each group is stacked separately, then OR-ed.
  • Short words: no truncation, plural added: if truncation would leave fewer than 5 letters (vein, line, cath) the word is kept whole, so nothing like cath* is produced; words of 4 letters or fewer get a plural added.
