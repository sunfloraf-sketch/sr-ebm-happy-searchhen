填寫說明How to fill in
．儲存格內：一詞一行（預設布林邏輯關係為 OR，不需標示）
．英文同義詞，請自行調整：(1) 單字切截 *；(2) 移除冗贅字詞（exercise 能找到 exercise therapy，不需列後者）；(3) 搭配鄰近字運算（參考載入範例寫法，或使用「鄰近字組合器」整理）
．控制詞彙：
‧ 不欲包含狹義詞時：請標註「不含狹義詞」、「不狹」、「noexp」或「no exp」，或直接勾選（點進控制詞彙格時會浮出標記小工具）
例：diabetes (不含狹義詞)、diabetes (noexp)
‧ 補充概念（藥物、化學物質、罕見疾病等不在標目樹中的詞）：驗證卡片按「標註為補充概念」，詞後會加 (補充概念：化學物質)；PubMed 以 [nm]、Ovid MEDLINE 以 .nm. 等欄位檢索，其他資料庫改放自由詞彙
‧ 單一儲存格內預設布林邏輯為 OR；如有特殊處理，請用 AND 及括號組合
例：
Optic Nerve
AND
(Ultrasonography
Optical Imaging)
‧ 主標題若附加副標題（subheading）：語法需自行查各資料庫
例 Ovid MEDLINE（dg 表示 Diagnostic Imaging）：
Optic Nerve/dg
．復原框內文字編輯：想還原剛才在某一格輸入框打的字，直接按 Ctrl+Z 即可。
• Within a cell: one term per line (the default Boolean is OR; no need to mark it).
• English synonyms — refine them yourself: (1) truncate word stems with *; (2) drop redundant terms (exercise already retrieves exercise therapy, so the latter isn't needed); (3) add proximity operators (see the loaded example, or tidy them in the Proximity Builder).
• Controlled vocabulary:
‧ To exclude narrower terms: mark it as non-exploded — 「不含狹義詞」, 「不狹」, noexp or no exp. Or simply tick (a small marking toolbar appears when you click into a controlled-vocabulary cell).
e.g. diabetes (不含狹義詞), diabetes (noexp)
‧ Supplementary concepts (drugs, chemicals, rare diseases outside the heading tree): use “Mark as supplementary concept” on the verification card, which appends (supplementary: chemical); PubMed searches it with [nm], Ovid MEDLINE with .nm. etc., and other databases get it as free text.
‧ The default Boolean within one cell is OR; for anything else, use AND and parentheses.
e.g.
Optic Nerve
AND
(Ultrasonography
Optical Imaging)
‧ To attach a subheading to a main heading: check each database for its own syntax
e.g. Ovid MEDLINE (dg = Diagnostic Imaging):
Optic Nerve/dg
• Undo in-cell text edits: to revert what you just typed in a cell, press Ctrl+Z.
