PICO 檢索策略產生器PICO Strategy Builder

PICO Strategy Builder

在 PICO 表填入/匯入自由詞彙與控制詞彙，啟動半自動查驗控制詞彙，選欄位決定完整度，一鍵生成各資料庫檢索策略 (可匯出)。並可用內建 PubMed API 粗估文章量、應用於實證精準查。Enter or import free-text terms and controlled vocabulary into the PICO table, run semi-automatic verification of the controlled vocabulary, choose a field level, and generate each database's strategy in one click (exportable). A built-in PubMed API can also estimate article counts, useful for precise evidence searching.

資料庫檢索語法轉換器Search Syntax Converter

Search Syntax Converter

貼上任一起始資料庫的檢索式，一鍵轉出各大資料庫／平台語法，並可下載 Word。（控制詞彙要自理喔！）Paste a query from any starting database and convert it to the major databases/platforms in one click, with Word export. (Controlled vocabulary is up to you!)

鄰近字分堆組合器Proximity Builder

Proximity Builder

把相同概念的多個片語，透過程式判斷自動分堆，再用鄰近字語法組合，兼顧完整與精準度的平衡。整理好可直接送進 PICO 產生器或語法轉換器。Phrases for the same concept are automatically grouped into stacks, then combined with proximity syntax to balance completeness and precision. Send the result straight to the PICO Strategy Builder or the Syntax Converter.

Happy SearchHen 緣起How Happy SearchHen began

系統性回顧的檢索，常讓人覺得繁瑣又容易出錯。
Happy SearchHen（搜尋策略生成雞）希望把這段過程變得友善一點：
你把同義詞與控制詞彙交給牠，
牠幫你把跨資料庫的檢索策略一顆一顆「生」出來
——穩定、可重現、好覆核。

Searching for a systematic review is often tedious and error-prone.
Happy SearchHen aims to make it a little friendlier:
hand over your synonyms and controlled vocabulary,
and it lays out cross-database search strategies one egg at a time
— stable, reproducible, and easy to double-check.

我幫你友善介面，你人道負責任養好雞
SearchHen 幫我們生出顆顆一致的金蛋 🥚

I make the interface friendly, you raise the hen humanely and responsibly,
and SearchHen lays consistent golden eggs for all of us 🥚

此工具包為純網頁程式，未使用到 AI 運作，僅在您的瀏覽器本機執行與暫存，不會上傳任何研究資料到網路。各工具輸出結果請人工覆核後再使用。This toolkit is pure web-based software with no AI — everything runs and is cached entirely in your browser and never uploads any of your research data. Please human-check each tool's output before use.
