鄰近字分堆闖關Proximity Stacking Quest

一個詞由兩個以上的字組成時，用鄰近字可以一次涵蓋各種寫法。幫生成雞把同義詞拆成詞堆，每關收集雞蛋！When a concept is written with two or more words, proximity searching catches all its variants at once. Help SearchHen split the synonyms into stacks and collect eggs on every level!

① 拖字分堆① Drag into stacks每一堆是鄰近關係的一個位置，堆內同義字用 OR 相連。也可以先點字、再點詞堆。Each stack is one position of the proximity relation; words in a stack are OR-ed. You can also tap a word, then tap a stack.
② 切截與取捨② Truncate or drop點字上的 ✂ 選擇寫法；縮寫、單一字放「單獨保留」，重複或已被涵蓋的字丟進「不需要」。Tap ✂ on a word to pick its form. Abbreviations and one-word terms go to “Keep separate”; repeated or already-covered words go to “Not needed”.
③ 檢查與加分題③ Check & bonus一次答對得 3 顆蛋，用提示或答錯會少蛋；過關後判斷組好的檢索式「找得到哪幾篇」，全對多拿一顆金蛋。Get it right first time for 3 eggs; hints and wrong checks cost eggs. Then decide which article titles your search would find — all correct wins a golden egg.
