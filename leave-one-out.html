揪出那個詞借用統合分析「逐一剔除敏感度分析」（leave-one-out sensitivity analysis）的概念，每個概念逐一拿掉一個詞，看每個詞帶進多少筆、哪個詞的獨有筆數最多，能幫助剔除或保留檢索詞，平衡檢索策略之周全與精準。（PubMed 可自動取得筆數）Leave-One-Out Term TestBorrowing the idea of the leave-one-out sensitivity analysis used in meta-analysis, it drops one term at a time from each concept to show how many records each term brings in and which adds the most unique records — helping you decide which terms to drop or keep, balancing sensitivity and precision. (PubMed counts can be fetched automatically.)

① 輸入① Input

提示：
  • 每個輸入框填寫一個概念，框內的詞為 OR 關係（用 OR 連接，或一行一個詞），概念之間以 AND 連接。
  • 片語詞彙輸入：可用雙引號"hand washing"；或一行寫成 NEAR／ADJ／AND 組合，如 (hand OR hands) AND (wash* OR rub)。
  • 欄位限定：需附加語法，否則依資料庫預設處理。
Tips:
  • Enter one concept per box; terms inside a box are OR-ed (separate with OR, or one per line), and concepts are AND-ed.
  • Phrases: use double quotes, e.g. "hand washing", or write a NEAR/ADJ/AND combination on one line, e.g. (hand OR hands) AND (wash* OR rub).
  • Field limits: add the field syntax yourself; otherwise the database default applies.

怎麼看結果Reading the results

類似統合分析的逐一剔除敏感度分析——一次拿掉一項，看整體結果改變多少；這裡拿掉的是檢索詞，看的是筆數變化，藉此判斷每個詞對檢索結果的影響程度。
點任一格筆數或填入，即複製那一版檢索式；手動模式貼到資料庫執行後，可人工填入筆數於該格（或資料庫畫面自行評估不回填）。
Like a leave-one-out sensitivity analysis in meta-analysis — remove one item at a time and see how much the overall result changes; here the item is a search term and the result is the record count, showing how much each term influences the search.
Click any count or enter cell to copy that search string; in manual mode, run it in the database and type the count into that cell (or just judge it on the database screen without entering it).

欄位定義Columns

  • 測試詞不加入時總數：這個詞所在的概念拿掉它，其餘照舊。
  • 測試詞獨有貢獻：〔完整檢索策略總數〕 − 〔測試詞不加入時總數〕，也就是只有這個詞才找得到的筆數。
    • 獨有貢獻多：讓總數暴增的詞彙。獨有多不代表該刪或該留，先到資料庫看那批獨有的文獻是不是雜訊，再決定刪、改寫或加限制。
    • 獨有貢獻少：這個詞找到的文獻大多已被其他詞涵蓋，保留或刪除影響都不大，大多狀況下可保留著。
    • 獨有貢獻 0：標示「已被涵蓋」，刪除沒影響，大多狀況下可保留著（若有兩個詞寫法相同時標「重複」）。
  • Total without test term: this term is removed from its concept; everything else unchanged.
  • test term unique: [full-strategy total] − [total without test term], i.e. records only this term finds.
    • High unique: the term inflating the total. A high unique count doesn’t mean the term should go or stay — check those unique records in the database for noise first, then decide whether to drop, rewrite or restrict it.
    • Low unique: most of what this term finds is already covered by other terms, so keeping or deleting it makes little difference; it can usually stay.
    • Unique = 0: marked “covered” — deleting it changes nothing, and it can usually stay (identical terms are marked “duplicate”).

系統如何測試詞彙How terms are tested

  • 所有概念的每個詞都會逐一測試；但若拿掉某個詞後，整個概念就空了，這個詞不測。
  • NEAR／ADJ／AND 組合內的每個詞也會拆開測試。
  • Every term in every concept is tested one by one; but if removing a term would leave its concept empty, that term is not tested.
  • Each word inside a NEAR/ADJ/AND combination is tested too.
